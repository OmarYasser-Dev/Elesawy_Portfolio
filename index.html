Case Study

Automated Product PDF Catalog

End-to-End Scraping & Document Pipeline
Website → Scraping → Pandas Clean → PDF Catalog
Problem

Manually collecting product information, prices, and images from e-commerce stores to format print/PDF catalogs is highly repetitive, prone to copy-paste errors, and takes hours of manual work per catalog.

Solution

An automated Python workflow that scrapes live product data and assets via Playwright & Requests, structures and validates records with Pandas, and generates publication-ready PDF catalogs via ReportLab with Arabic RTL support.

Result & Impact

Reduced catalog generation time from 4+ hours of manual assembly down to under 2 minutes of automated execution, with 100% data accuracy and consistent professional layout.

Core Capabilities:

  • Automated grid layout with dynamic product cards
  • Dynamic image downloading and proportional embedding
  • Full Arabic text reshaping & right-to-left (RTL) support
  • Dynamic currency formatting and catalog pagination
